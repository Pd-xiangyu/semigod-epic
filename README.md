The Demigod System: A Theoretical Framework for Cognitive and Productivity Synergistic Emergence based on Human-Supra-Loop (Vol. I: The Demigod Paradigm)
Abstract
Artificial intelligence technology is evolving at an exponentially accelerating pace, with human strongholds being conquered one after another, sparking widespread societal anxiety. This paper directly analyzes and responds to these concerns, reversing the traditional path of "AI application" to explore a novel usage of large models for "intelligence augmentation". This paper redefines the concept of a "Demigod", defining it as a new-era Marxist practitioner who balances rationality and emotion, masters the paradigm of human-machine symbiosis, and skillfully employs high-context narratives to harness intelligence augmentation. It establishes the formula "AI-in-the-Loop" + "Human-on-the-Supra-Loop" = "The Demigod Paradigm (Prototype)". This paradigm posits the human as the absolute leader, maximizing and augmenting human innovative capacity through high-frequency, deep mutual referencing between human and machine within high-context settings, thereby achieving a synergistic emergence of cognition and productivity. All viewpoints and innovations are proposed by the human, and all decisions are controlled by the human, thus making the innovation process visible. Unlike AI applications, the Demigod Paradigm is about human innovation, where AI serves merely as a "mirror" to assist cognition. Humans critically reconstruct AI outputs with rigorous standards, rejecting simple replication to ensure the subjectivity and originality of the final outcome. This paper returns to humanism, and the proposed "Demigod Paradigm" achieves a historical transcendence and fundamental subversion of the existing "strong application, weak innovation" human-machine interaction model.
Keywords
Human-on-the-Supra-Loop; Intelligence Augmentation; Demigod Paradigm; Synergistic Emergence; Innovation Process Visualization
